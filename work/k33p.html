Building trust without hiding responsibility.

A clear identity for a self-custody product that gives users control and makes the consequences understandable.

K33P

The brand was developed alongside the product, giving K33P one clear position and a coherent identity system from the start.

Product direction

K33P asks people to hold their own keys. That freedom is central to the product, but so is the consequence: there is no institution to reverse a mistake or restore access.

Confidence through consistency.

The identity makes consequential moments feel considered, legible and connected without borrowing the false reassurance of custodial platforms.

Positioning, tone and visual identity were shaped in step with the product so the brand responded to what K33P was becoming rather than describing it from a distance.
