Turning a market call into a record.

A brand system for a skill-based crypto prediction market built around directional calls, measurable accuracy and onchain settlement.

Brail

Brail turns a short-term market opinion into something that can be measured. Traders commit to Up or Down at a live entry price, choose a horizon and let the call settle automatically.

The product removes order books and synthetic odds from the core decision. One market, one direction, one time horizon and settlement against live prices. The identity needed to make that structure feel credible without borrowing the usual language of speculation.

One call, fixed in time.

Each prediction locks the selected asset, exchange price and horizon at entry. Correct calls earn points, while weekly USDC and OAR fees fund separate reward pools distributed according to performance.

Primary identity
Directional mark

The mark holds the mechanic.

Two opposing directional forms frame a central point: Up or Down, fixed around the price at entry. The hexagonal form gives the symbol an onchain structure and lets each call read as one unit in a growing record.

Rendered in metal, glass, moss and terrazzo, the mark moves from interface signal to tangible object. The form stays recognisable as its surface changes.

From opinion to evidence.

The campaign idea challenges traders to move beyond the confidence of a take and build a visible history of decisions instead.

The communication system uses blunt, declarative language. It does not promise perfect calls; it asks traders to make a decision, accept a horizon and build evidence over time.

One line, different market moments.

“Let your record do the talking” became a focused campaign family. Changing the people and setting extends the same idea across different traders without changing its visual voice.

Communication at market speed.

The same hierarchy supports maintenance notices, weekly results, platform milestones and leaderboard updates. Acid green marks the information that needs attention while black keeps dense updates controlled.

Built for the moments around the product.

Launch, public testing, reward cycles and operational updates all needed to feel like one brand. The interface is shown here as context; my remit focused on the strategy, identity and communication system surrounding it.

The resulting system gives Brail a consistent way to explain a new market behaviour: every call is time-bound, every result is settled and every trader can build a record that speaks for itself.
